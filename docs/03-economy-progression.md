# Economy, Progression, and Monetization

## 1. Principles

1. Combat power is never purchased, earned through grinding, or randomized.
2. Every player starts with identical statistics and complete access to the competitive launch Node set.
3. Spending changes expression and collection, not hitboxes, timing, readability, matchmaking, rewards, or rating.
4. Prices are shown directly; no loot boxes.
5. Free rewards remain meaningful without making absence feel punitive.
6. Systems are added only after core-match validation.

## 2. Currencies

### Signals — soft currency

Earned through normal play. Used for evergreen cosmetics, profile items, selected recolors, and non-power collection goals.

Sources:

- first matches of the day;
- daily and weekly missions;
- account journey;
- free Pass track;
- achievements;
- duplicate-free event conversion where needed.

There is a daily boosted earning window, not a hard earning cap. Continued play still earns a smaller amount.

### Cores — hard currency

Purchased with money and occasionally granted in small, disclosed amounts through the Premium Pass. Used for premium cosmetics, Pass access, and bundles. It cannot buy Signals where doing so would imply gameplay acceleration, because there is no gameplay power to accelerate.

Prices are localized and finalized only after platform fees, taxes, and regional purchasing power are reviewed.

### Shards — seasonal event currency

Earned from a specific event and used in its event shop. At event end:

- a clearly disclosed grace period allows spending;
- unspent Shards convert to Signals at a published rate;
- no paid Shard packs during the initial economy.

This avoids stranded value and manipulative expiration.

## 3. Reward budgets

Provisional non-monetary pacing:

| Activity | Signals |
|---|---:|
| First completed match/day | 100 |
| Second completed match/day | 60 |
| Third completed match/day | 40 |
| Further completed match | 10 |
| Daily mission | 60–100 |
| Weekly mission | 250–400 |
| First weekly Ranked placement | 300 |
| Free Pass milestone | 100–300 |

Example evergreen prices:

| Item | Signals |
|---|---:|
| Basic profile icon | 600 |
| Banner colorway | 900 |
| Common emote | 1,500 |
| Node recolor set | 2,500 |
| Arena accent set | 4,000 |
| Featured evergreen Avatar skin | 7,500 |

These values are initial hypotheses. Target time-to-first-choice is 1–2 sessions; target time for a desirable evergreen skin is 2–4 weeks of moderate play.

## 4. Account journey

Account level represents engagement, never combat strength.

- Levels grant Signals, icons, banners, tutorials, statistics views, and occasional evergreen cosmetics.
- Match XP depends primarily on completion and participation, with a modest victory bonus.
- Surrender or AFK abuse reduces rewards.
- No purchasable XP boosters at launch.
- No level-gated Nodes in Ranked.

## 5. Missions

### Daily

Three choices from low-friction goals, with one free reroll:

- complete two matches;
- activate three different Node families;
- create two combos;
- prevent 20 damage;
- play one Flow match.

Daily missions avoid “win X matches,” specific paid cosmetics, or harmful play patterns. One reserve slot preserves an unfinished daily for 24 hours.

### Weekly

Longer goals reinforce breadth:

- complete 20 matches;
- activate every launch Node;
- play both Quick and Ranked;
- share or save one replay;
- complete the weekly seeded challenge.

### Achievements

Achievements reward durable accomplishments and surprising interactions. Secret achievements must not require purchases. Competitive achievements do not encourage deranking or griefing.

## 6. Login and streaks

A seven-step **activity path** advances on any five days within a rolling seven-day window. Missing one day does not reset progress.

Rewards include Signals, profile cosmetics, and Pass XP. The final reward is desirable but not exclusive forever. This supports return behavior without punishment-heavy streak design.

## 7. Seasonal Pass

Provisional season length: 8 weeks.

- Free and Premium tracks share one XP meter.
- 50 core tiers plus repeatable cosmetic-neutral post-tiers.
- Premium purchase grants already-earned Premium rewards retroactively.
- Missions persist long enough to catch up.
- No combat power, currency gambling, or paid skip required to complete.
- Tier skips may be sold only after real completion pacing is known and never affect Ranked access.

Suggested contents:

- Free: Signals, icons, banners, emotes, one thematic cosmetic set.
- Premium: Avatar skins, arena skin, Node appearance set, landing trail, KO presentation, victory presentation, premium currency, title.

The Pass should be completable with approximately 4–5 hours per week across the season, subject to telemetry.

## 8. Cosmetic catalogue

- Avatar skins;
- Arena skins;
- Node skins;
- jump trails and animations;
- particle accents;
- KO and victory presentations;
- emotes and sprays;
- banners, icons, and profile frames;
- UI color themes;
- seasonal bundles.

### Readability rules

- canonical silhouette and landing time never change;
- enemy and friendly states retain standardized outlines;
- Node family icon and cooldown remain fixed;
- VFX intensity respects accessibility settings;
- premium cosmetics cannot disguise selection, damage, warning, or status information.

## 9. Shop

- direct purchase, no loot boxes;
- clear Cores and approximate local-money price;
- daily featured presentation may rotate, but evergreen items remain discoverable;
- limited items show exact end time and whether they may return;
- no false discounts or personalized combat-related pricing;
- parental controls and purchase confirmation follow platform requirements;
- purchase restoration is supported.

The daily shop is a merchandising surface, not the only access path to owned content.

## 10. Social and referral rewards

Friend invitations and referrals award cosmetics or Signals after genuine account milestones. Safeguards include account age, verified match completion, device/risk signals, reward limits, and reversal on fraud.

Neither inviter nor invitee receives competitive power. Private matches cannot efficiently farm normal progression.

## 11. Ranked and leaderboard rewards

- seasonal participation reward;
- tier-based title, banner accent, or profile frame;
- top-rank cosmetic is commemorative, never statistically different;
- weekly leaderboard provides recognition and modest Signals, not exclusive power;
- rewards are based on finalized standings after anti-cheat review.

## 12. Retention design

### Day 1

- playable within seconds as guest;
- first meaningful decision in under one minute;
- guided short match;
- immediate cosmetic choice;
- visible but not overwhelming next goal;
- one-tap rematch.

### Day 7

- weekly challenge;
- unlocked match history and richer statistics;
- first seasonal cosmetic milestone;
- friend invitation;
- introduction to Ranked after sufficient rule comprehension.

### Day 30

- season arc and rank identity;
- collection goals;
- rotating event rules;
- replay mastery;
- clan/social goals only after healthy player population exists.

## 13. Economy health metrics

- Signals earned, spent, and stored by cohort;
- first purchase time and conversion;
- cosmetic ownership and equip rates;
- Pass completion distribution;
- mission completion and reroll rates;
- store impressions versus purchases;
- payer/non-payer retention and match outcomes;
- refund and accidental purchase rates;
- currency liability;
- percentage of players who perceive monetization as fair.

## 14. Economy guardrails

- no paid stat, cooldown, Node, protocol, matchmaking, or information advantage;
- no energy system limiting match play;
- no randomized paid rewards;
- no duplicate cosmetic purchases;
- no expiration of purchased goods except clearly defined licensed constraints;
- no dark patterns around streak loss;
- changes to paid item value require transparent policy and support process.

## 15. Live-ops cadence

Initial sustainable cadence:

- daily: mission refresh and shop presentation;
- weekly: seeded challenge and leaderboard;
- biweekly: event modifier or curated playlist;
- every 8 weeks: season, Pass, balance window, cosmetics;
- major Node additions only after testing and usually outside an active Ranked ruleset.

Content cadence expands only when production capacity and player population justify it.
